charter-regular.woff2 and charter-bold.woff2: Bitstream Charter, converted from the Type 1 outline fonts
(font-bitstream-type1) to OpenType/WOFF2 and subset to Latin; the outlines, widths and kerning are unchanged.
They set the serif of the sample news site in the home scene "sites" where the system has no serif of its own
listed before them (on a Mac, Safari uses New York and Chrome Iowan Old Style or Charter; these files are not
downloaded there).
