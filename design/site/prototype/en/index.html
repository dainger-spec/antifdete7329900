The Safari window of the Store profile. A sign-in page is open through the proxy; during the next load the proxy stops answering, and instead of the page Safari shows its own error, “Can’t Connect to the Server”. The profile never goes online directly.

Never around it. A profile with a proxy goes online only through it. If the proxy doesn’t answer, the page doesn’t open, and the site never learns your real IP.

0 packets around the proxy. Every build checks it: the tests record all of a profile’s traffic and look for a single packet that went around.

Not even we can read it.

Passwords, cookies, history, bookmarks and site data of every profile are encrypted on disk.

The key comes from your master password, and only you know it. Without it, a stopped profile can’t be opened.

In the picture a line of light passes over a profile’s data — a saved password, cookies, history, a bookmark and site data — and turns it into ciphertext, which then gathers into a lock.
