Lumen is a browser of isolated sessions for testing websites: each session has its own WebKit data store (WKWebsiteDataStore(forIdentifier:)) and, optionally, its own HTTP or SOCKS5 proxy.

How to review:
1. Without signing in, the app works on the free level: create a session (+), open it, browse.
2. To see proxies: Account (top left) > Sign in with the demo account below. Then Proxies (top left) > + and paste the demo proxy below; open a session's settings (slider icon in its bottom bar) and choose that proxy.

Demo account and demo proxy: provided with the submission (App Store Connect > App Review Information), not in this file.

The app has no sales inside it and no links to sales outside it. A Lumen account is created and managed on the web; the app only signs in to it (guideline 3.1.3(f)).
