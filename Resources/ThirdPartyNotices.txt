Lumen — third-party notices

================================================================================================
OpenVPN connections and VLESS, VMess, Trojan and Shadowsocks links
================================================================================================

A release of Lumen carries three more programs for these (a build made with OVPN=0 or XRAY=0 lacks
the ones it names): openvpn and LumenXray are other projects' programs, which Lumen starts and does
not link into its own code; LumenTunnel is Lumen's own, with lwIP in it.

OpenVPN connections, with the license texts and the source information they need in
Contents/Resources/ThirdParty/OpenVPN:

- openvpn (Contents/MacOS/openvpn): OpenVPN 2.7 (https://openvpn.net), GPL-2.0, built unmodified
  with mbed TLS (Apache-2.0 OR GPL-2.0-or-later, used under the GPL), LZO (GPL-2.0-or-later) and
  LZ4 (BSD-2-Clause). It is a separate program that Lumen starts; SOURCES.txt there says where its
  complete source is.
- LumenTunnel (Contents/MacOS/LumenTunnel): Lumen's own, with lwIP
  (https://savannah.nongnu.org/projects/lwip/), Copyright (c) 2001-2004 Swedish Institute of
  Computer Science, BSD-3-Clause (lwIP-COPYING.txt there).

Links: LumenXray (Contents/MacOS/LumenXray) is Xray-core (https://github.com/XTLS/Xray-core),
Mozilla Public License 2.0, built unmodified from its source. Contents/Resources/ThirdParty/Xray/
NOTICE.txt says which version and commit, where its source is, and holds the license of every Go
module built into it and of the Go standard library.
